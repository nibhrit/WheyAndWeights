# packaged-in.json — licence

`packaged-in.json` is a database of packaged food products sold in India, extracted and cleaned from
**Open Food Facts** (https://world.openfoodfacts.org) by `build-packaged.mjs`.

- The database is made available under the **Open Database License (ODbL) 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/
- Individual contents are under the **Database Contents License (DbCL) 1.0**: https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/dbcl/1-0/
- Source: Open Food Facts contributors.

It is kept separate from the app's own food list (`app/src/data/foods/foods.json`, USDA FoodData Central, CC0).
